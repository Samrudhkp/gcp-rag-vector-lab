# Acme Corp Expense Policy (excerpt)

## Expense reimbursement
Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date.
Meals while traveling are capped at $75 per day without manager pre-approval.
Alcohol is not reimbursable except for approved customer entertainment.

## Travel
Book flights and hotels through the corporate travel portal.
Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours.
Rental cars require manager approval for trips inside the same metro area.

## Software subscriptions
Software under $50/month can be expensed with manager Slack approval.
Software over $50/month requires a purchase order via Finance.

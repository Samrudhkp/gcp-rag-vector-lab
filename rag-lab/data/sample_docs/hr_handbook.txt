# Acme Corp HR Handbook (excerpt)

## Vacation policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year.
Vacation must be requested at least 5 business days in advance in Workday.
Unused vacation does not roll over except up to 5 days with manager approval.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive 8 weeks.
Notify HR at least 30 days before the expected leave start date when possible.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 in the employee's local timezone.

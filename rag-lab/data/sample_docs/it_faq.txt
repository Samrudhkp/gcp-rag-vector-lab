# Acme Corp IT FAQ

## Password reset
Employees can reset their password at https://okta.acme.example/reset.
Use your work email. A reset link expires after 30 minutes.
If you are locked out after 5 failed attempts, contact IT Support on Slack #it-help.

## VPN access
Install the company VPN client from the Software Center.
Connect to vpn.acme.example using your Okta credentials and MFA.
Remote contractors must use the contractor VPN profile named "acme-ext".

## Laptop setup
New hires receive a laptop on day one. Sign in with Okta, then open the
"New Hire Checklist" in Notion. Encrypt the disk with FileVault (Mac) or BitLocker (Windows).
